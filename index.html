Studio Publisher

A private, owner-operated desktop tool for preparing approved Etsy digital-product listings and Pinterest video Pins. This public website contains app information and its privacy policy, not the private application, account credentials or product files.

What the app does

  • Prepares exact-content publishing plans and obtains the operator's approval.
  • Uses official OAuth/API connections rather than account passwords or browser cookies.
  • Uploads the operator's selected listing files and creates approved promotional Pins.
  • Records operation outcomes and retrieves requested aggregate Pinterest metrics.

Connections and publishing depend on platform access, account consent and the applicable release requirements. The app is currently for its operator's own accounts, not a public multi-user service.
